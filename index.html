✦ About Us

Team Thay Cung / Exorcist Studio

Who We Are

Team Thay Cung (Exorcist Studio) is a Vietnamese boardgame design studio dedicated to creating games that celebrate and explore Vietnamese spiritual culture, folklore, and rituals.

Our Mission

We bring Vietnamese spiritual heritage closer to the world through immersive tabletop and digital games. We believe folklore is not just history-it's an endless source of storytelling, strategy, and human connection. Through our games, we honor ancestral traditions while creating modern, engaging experiences.

Our Vision

To establish Vietnamese boardgame design as a distinctive voice in the global gaming industry, rooted in authentic cultural narratives and ritual aesthetics. We aspire to export Vietnamese spiritual worldviews through games that are mechanically rich, narratively deep, and culturally resonant.

About the Game

Become a Spirit Commander Exorcist with the power to capture demons and use sacred talismans to fight both evil spirits and rival exorcists in a cursed ancient village. Spirit Gathering is a strategic 16+ boardgame for 3-6 players, inspired by Vietnam's mystical spiritual world.

Step into the mystical world of Spirit Gathering, where the boundary between the living and the spirit realm gradually collapses. In a cursed ancient village, evil spirits have broken free from their seals, spreading fear and darkness over all living creatures. As Spirit Commander Exorcists, players must use wisdom, tactics, and spiritual power to capture demons, deploy traditional talismans, and summon spirit soldiers to gain control of the spirit realm.

Each player possesses a unique set of skills, talismans, and strategies, creating dramatic mind games between exorcists. Players must not only confront dangerous evil spirits roaming the village, but also compete with each other to become the most powerful exorcist.

Key Features

  • Law of Life and Death: Unlike many card boardgames where death means elimination, Spirit Gathering allows players to resurrect after dying. This reduces early "out of game" frustration while creating dramatic comeback moments even at the brink of defeat. Inspired by Vietnamese reincarnation beliefs, death is not an endpoint but a transitional phase-spirits can be reborn carrying karma and influence from their previous life.
  • Vietnamese Spiritual Legends: Spirit Gathering brings players into Vietnam's spiritual world, where folk legend spirits and demons are vividly recreated through unique visual design, skills, and mechanics for each entity. Every demon carries its own story and cultural element-for example, Thần Trùng possesses the "Trùng Tang" effect, inspired by folk beliefs about consecutive deaths within a family.
  • Spirit Commander Exorcist Roleplay: One of Spirit Gathering's standout features is its strong roleplay experience, where players become Spirit Commander Exorcists with the power to capture, subdue, and command demons. Through tactical planning, charm usage, and spirit control, players don't just fight to survive-they gradually expand their own spiritual authority.
  • Deep Vietnamese Cultural Identity: Unlike many strategy boardgames inspired by Western culture, Spirit Gathering builds its world on Vietnamese folk beliefs and spiritual legends-talismans, spirit soldiers, reincarnation, and spiritual entities all developed from indigenous culture.
  • High Interactive Gameplay: Designed around continuous player interaction-from disrupting opponents' strategies and competing for spirits to using talismans to shift the battlefield, every action can directly affect the entire table.

Lore & World Overview

Deep within the forests and remote mountains of ancient Vietnam lies a forgotten village where the boundary between the underworld and the living realm gradually collapses. Once protected by guardian exorcists, the village fell into chaos after a forbidden ritual accidentally shattered the seal separating the spirit world.

From that moment, vengeful souls, evil spirits, and demons began to appear, bringing plague, omens, and mysterious deaths. The dead could not transcend, while the living existed in fear of ever-growing supernatural forces.

To prevent catastrophe, exorcists summoned Spirit Gathering-an army of souls bound by talismans and spiritual contracts. But such power always comes at a price. The more they wield the underworld's strength, the more exorcists are eroded by evil energy and gradually lose themselves.

In Spirit Gathering, players become Spirit Commander Exorcists entering the cursed village to subdue spirits and command demons. But amid the battle against evil spirits, exorcists must also confront each other to seize supreme power between the two realms of life and death-and become the strongest exorcist.

Inspired by Vietnamese folk beliefs and spiritual legends, Spirit Gathering delivers a mystical world where death is not the end, but only the beginning of a new cycle of reincarnation.

FAQ

How many players is the game for?

Spirit Gathering is designed for groups of 3-6 players, and becomes more engaging as more exorcists join the table.

How long does a game of Spirit Gathering last?

Each game is expected to last 15-45 phút depending on the number of players and strategies used.

Why did you take inspiration from Vietnamese culture?

We wanted to create a boardgame with a distinct Vietnamese identity rather than using familiar Western settings. Vietnamese spiritual culture and folk legends offer many unique elements perfectly suited for a tactical, roleplay-driven boardgame world. We also hope to introduce Vietnamese culture to domestic and international boardgame communities in a modern, accessible way.

Why does the game have a resurrection mechanic?

The resurrection mechanic in Spirit Gathering is built on the concept of reincarnation in Vietnamese spiritual culture, where death is not an absolute end but a transitional phase. In gameplay, this keeps players from being eliminated, removes the frustration of waiting for a new game, and creates opportunities for dramatic comebacks-keeping matches tense, competitive, and interactive until the very end.

What makes Spirit Gathering different from other boardgames?

Spirit Gathering stands out through its exploration of Vietnamese folk beliefs and spiritual culture, combined with resurrection mechanics, spirit subjugation, and demon control-creating a unique tactical experience rich in roleplay.

Is Spirit Gathering accessible to new boardgame players?

Spirit Gathering is designed so both newcomers and experienced boardgamers can enjoy it. The rule system is clear but offers enough tactical depth for high competitiveness and replayability.

Where can players follow the latest updates?

Follow our Facebook fanpage or visit exorciststudio.com for updates on development progress and release timing.

Has the game been released yet?

Spirit Gathering is currently in publisher partnership with Everjoy Publishing, with a planned release window of 2026-2027 (TBA).
