✦ About Us

Team Thay Cung / Exorcist Studio

Who We Are

Team Thay Cung (Exorcist Studio) is a Vietnamese boardgame design studio dedicated to creating games that celebrate and explore Vietnamese spiritual culture, folklore, and rituals.

Our Mission

We bring Vietnamese spiritual heritage closer to the world through immersive tabletop and digital games. We believe folklore is not just history-it's an endless source of storytelling, strategy, and human connection. Through our games, we honor ancestral traditions while creating modern, engaging experiences.

Our Vision

To establish Vietnamese boardgame design as a distinctive voice in the global gaming industry, rooted in authentic cultural narratives and ritual aesthetics. We aspire to export Vietnamese spiritual worldviews through games that are mechanically rich, narratively deep, and culturally resonant.

Elevator Pitch

Am Binh: The Spirit Wardens is a strategic card boardgame where 3–6 players command ghostly armies across the Vietnamese spirit realm. Through tactical positioning, ritual mechanics, and supernatural strategy, players outwit rivals to control territories and influence the boundary between the living and dead. Rooted in Vietnamese folklore, every game is a séance-every decision, a prayer.

About the Game

Am Binh immerses players in the liminal realm between life and death-a space of Vietnamese spiritual mythology where ghosts, demons, and ancestral forces negotiate territory and destiny.

Players assume the role of spirit wardens, summoning and commanding units across a ritualistic game board. Through clever card play, strategic positioning, and knowledge of spirit hierarchies, wardens compete to dominate regions and fulfill spiritual contracts.

The game celebrates Vietnamese supernatural lore while delivering complex, rewarding mechanics that encourage interaction, bluffing, and emergent storytelling. Winner is determined not only by territory control but also by the fulfillment of spiritual obligations and ritual mastery.

Key Features

  • Ritual Mechanics: Unique gameplay systems rooted in Vietnamese ancestor veneration, spirit summoning, and ritual obligation.
  • Strategic Depth: Complex card interactions, area control, and economic systems that reward tactical thinking and long-term planning.
  • Cultural Authenticity: Lore and mechanics deeply researched and inspired by actual Vietnamese spiritual traditions, not superficial aesthetics.
  • Emergent Storytelling: Every game generates unique narrative moments through player interaction, spirit summoning, and territory disputes.
  • Scalable Complexity: Accessible to new players while offering strategic depth for experienced gamers.
  • Beautiful Component Design: Premium artwork, card stock, and component design that evokes the dark, mysterious spirit world.
  • Replayability: Multiple faction options, random event cards, and strategic variety ensure no two games play identically.
  • Social Experience: Designed for memorable table moments-laughter, tension, and camaraderie.

Lore & World Overview

In Vietnamese spiritual tradition, the boundary between the living and the dead is not a wall but a negotiation. Ancestors watch over their descendants. Spirits demand respect and ritual. Demons seek power. The living tend to both-through offerings, prayers, and séances.

Am Binh (literally "Spirit Soldiers") explores this liminal space. Players assume roles as spirit wardens-intermediaries between worlds who command armies of ghosts, demons, and ancestral forces. The game board represents contested spiritual territory: shrines, temples, and burial grounds where the living and dead vie for influence.

Victory is not conquest alone-it is the fulfillment of spiritual obligations, the honoring of ritual, and the mastery of the delicate balance between veneration and control.

How to Play (Overview)

Setup: Each player takes a faction (each rooted in a different Vietnamese spirit tradition) and begins with a ritual hand of cards and spirit units.

Gameplay Loop: On your turn, you may:

  • Play spirit cards to summon units onto contested territory
  • Move existing spirits to claim or defend regions
  • Perform rituals to activate special abilities and fulfill spiritual contracts
  • Interact with other players' spirits through negotiation or conflict

Victory Conditions: Win by controlling key shrines, fulfilling spirit contracts, and maintaining spiritual balance. The player who masters both territory and ritual emerges as the supreme spirit warden.
